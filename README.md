# Rucoy-Fishing-Mastery
Fishing game made for browser completely free using A.i
What's new:

**🔊 SOUND SYSTEM**
- Every action now has sound — casting, catching, wrong buttons, fleeing, line breaking, buying items
- Fish caught sound **scales with rarity** — master fish sounds epic!
- Level up has a full fanfare melody

**🌤️ WEATHER SOUNDS**
- All 13 weather types have unique ambient sounds
- Rain + thunder rumbles, snowy howling wind, magical chimes, cosmic drone, eclipse ominous hum, eruption crackle and more
- Changes automatically when weather changes

**🗺️ MAP SOUNDS**
- Each of the 7 maps has its own water and nature soundscape
- Plays in the background while you fish

**🪱 BAIT SYSTEM (new tab)**
- 10 bait types from basic Earthworm to rare Star Dust
- Each bait attracts specific fish — use the right bait for better results
- Limited stock per bait type
- Vending machine sound when buying

**🍱 FOOD & BEVERAGES (new tab)**
- 14 items — Green Tea, Ramen, Onigiri, Sandwich, Apple, Energy Drink and more
- Each has a unique eating sound — crunchy crackers, slurping ramen, blowing hot tea!
- Restores Stamina and/or HP
- Limited stock per item

**⚡ STAMINA SYSTEM**
- Max 1440 stamina = 24 hours worth
- Each cast costs 10 stamina
- Recovers automatically while you are offline
- Stamina bar shown at the top of the screen

**❤️ HP SYSTEM**
- Shown at top of screen
- Restore by eating food or resting at the Inn

**🏨 INN SYSTEM (new tab)**
- Every map has its own Inn with different name, price and luxury
- Riverside 500G → Celestial Abyss 8000G
- Each Inn has unique relaxing spa sounds
- Resting restores Stamina and HP based on Inn quality

**😱 SCREAM TO SKIP REST**
- While resting at an Inn, tap the Scream button
- It uses your phone microphone
- Scream loud enough and it cuts 5 hours off your rest time
- Costs 500G per scream attempt

**💾 SAVE SYSTEM**
- Saves all new data including stamina, HP, bait, food, inn rest progress
- Auto saves every 30 seconds and after every major time
